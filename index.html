The personal information we collect may include:

  • Name and contact data (email, Discord username, Roblox username)
  • Project details, game links, and metrics you provide in forms
  • Communication data (messages you send us)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and pages viewed.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted.
